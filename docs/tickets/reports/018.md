## Acceptance evidence

`tests/audio/ticket18-m4-sound-quality.test.ts` renders the production
`EngineAudioProcessor` with a 0.5-second warm-up followed by 32,768 measured
frames in 128-frame blocks. The six 48 kHz rows hold RPM `4800`, load `10 N m`,
throttle `0.45`, reference gain `0.5`, and seed `0x18000001` constant so preset
differences are controlled. The 44.1 kHz endpoint rows keep those same controls
for the one- and four-cylinder presets. The nominal RPM is also the product
dynamics' starting and idle-control target; final telemetry RPM must remain
within 5%, rather than being presented as a perfect fixed-speed oscillator.

The defined automated limits are: all PCM and metrics finite, RMS in
`[0.00001, 0.5]`, absolute DC at most `0.02`, protected peak at most `1`,
combustion-order energy ratio at least `0.0001`, top-10%-of-band Nyquist guard
at most `-40 dB`, and observed RPM within 5% of the nominal value. Ticket 17's
separate near-redline product guard remains stricter at `-45 dB`. Every full-mix
and combustion probe must also emit `config-applied` and `ready` with the actual
row sample rate and remain free of `fatal-error`.

RMS, DC, peak, and Nyquist guard are measured from the full M4 mix. The
combustion-order ratio is measured through a second production-processor render
with only additive intake noise and mechanical gain disabled. This retains the
combustion/exhaust path while preventing the configured crank orders from
satisfying the combustion-specific gate.

The same test measures 1,000 product-processor blocks after a 250-block warm-up
for the four-cylinder 48 kHz row. The latest full-suite p99 was `0.128 ms`, below the

harness measurement, not a browser AudioWorklet profiler result.

## Review

- Self-review covered matrix completeness, actual product-path use, warm-up and
  analysis windows, FFT butterfly correctness, finite/protected output,
  spectral thresholds, dynamic RPM disclosure, and performance-test scope.
- Self-review found and fixed an FFT butterfly that reused an overwritten real
  component. It also tightened the minimum combustion-order ratio from
  `0.000001` to `0.0001` and RPM error from 15% to 5% after calibration.
- Independent Terra high review found three acceptance defects and one
  lower-severity contract gap: mechanical crank orders could satisfy the
  combustion-order gate, native processor faults were not rejected, preset
  rows changed multiple comparison controls at once, and the reported runtime
  sample rate was not asserted from the Worklet handshake. Corrections added a
  combustion/exhaust-only product probe, explicit ready/fatal checks, all six
  presets at common controls, and 44.1 kHz endpoint rows. Reviewer recheck
  confirmed the isolated probe and all corrections, with no remaining
  actionable findings.

## Limits and next actions

- Every preset value and comparison coefficient is a synthetic model value, not
  measured real-engine data. No real-vehicle equivalence is claimed.
- The Node harness verifies deterministic offline PCM and timing headroom; it
  does not prove physical-device latency, freedom from audible dropouts, or
  perceived sound quality in a browser.
- Optional listening evidence is not required for `Status: verified` and was not
  performed: `listening: not-run`.
- A future listening entry must keep the evidence fields separate:
  `実施者`, `ブラウザとバージョン`, `device`, `出力経路`, `matrix row`, and
  `所見（音切れ / 耳障りな alias / preset 差）`. An empty or `not-run` entry
  must not be interpreted as a pass.
- Independent review, committed local gates, and current-head GitHub CI remain
  pending at this draft stage.
