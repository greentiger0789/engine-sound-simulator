  and irregularly partitioned renders, different PCM for different seeds, finite

- `tests/audio/m4-resonance-regression.test.ts` exercises variation-only,
  mechanical-only, both-enabled, and both-disabled product Worklet renders at
  44.1 and 48 kHz. Each combination is restart-reproducible, finite, free of fatal
  processor messages, and has absolute peak at or below 1. It also proves that a
  changed seed changes PCM and that reapplying the same seed matches a fresh
  processor.
- `tests/audio/mechanical-orders.test.ts` verifies deterministic reset, render
  partition invariance, bounded finite output at 44.1/48 kHz, changed PCM for
  changed orders, atomic rejection, and a bounded order count/value contract.
- The mechanical spectrum case fixes 6000 rpm at 48 kHz for one second. A second
  crank order appears at 200 Hz, with its DFT magnitude greater than both 199 Hz
  and 201 Hz by more than 100 times.
- Fixed comparison conditions used by the product regression are seed `101`,
  throttle `0.7`, dynamometer load `20 N m`, and 44.1/48 kHz. Presets use seed
  `0x16c0ffee`, amplitude variation `0.06`, width variation `0.04`, mechanical
  gain `0.035`, and orders 1 and 2.

  seeds, variation bounds, mechanical gain, order values, and non-finite values.
